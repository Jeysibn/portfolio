🚀 What I Do

I am actively designing and implementing DevOps solutions that bridge the gap between development and operations. My core focus is on continuous upskilling to build automated, secure systems that make software delivery faster and remarkably reliable. Here is how I am applying my toolkit:

CI/CD Pipelines

I streamline the path to production. Using GitHub Actions, I build automated testing and deployment workflows that eliminate manual bottlenecks and ensure consistent delivery.

Infrastructure as Code

I treat environments like software. By leveraging Terraform, I provision and manage cloud resources through version-controlled code, making deployments predictable, repeatable, and easily scalable.

Containerization & Orchestration

I build for modern, portable architecture. I use Docker to package applications for consistency across environments, and Kubernetes to orchestrate, scale, and securely manage complex microservices.

Cloud Architecture (AWS & Azure)

I design secure, resilient cloud infrastructure. I have hands-on experience structuring environments, isolating networks, and deploying compute using core services like AWS (EC2, VPC, Security Groups, Subnets, S3) and Azure (VNET, VM, Subnets, Function App).

Monitoring & Logging

I believe deep visibility is the key to reliability. I use Prometheus and Grafana to monitor system performance in real-time, visualize metrics, and proactively troubleshoot issues before they escalate.

📖 My Journey

My engineering journey began with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from National University Baliwag, driven by a simple question: how do complex systems operate reliably at global scale?

Building on that foundation, I started in hands-on IT support and progressed to working as a Technical Support Engineer at TrendAI. Troubleshooting real-world system issues, managing security workloads, and assisting users taught me two critical lessons: systems need to be resilient, and manual fixes don't scale.

That realization pushed me toward DevOps and Cloud Architecture. Rather than just supporting systems after they break, I focused on automating their deployment and infrastructure from the start. Today, backed by certifications in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and GitHub, I am dedicated to mastering the intersection of software and cloud infrastructure. I approach every challenge with a strict growth mindset—treating every bottleneck as a learning opportunity and every manual task as a candidate for automation.
